# References and Scholarly Literature

### Primary Regulatory & Operational Sources
- **[1] Electrify America.** (2024). *Electrify America Charging FAQ: Understanding Idle Fees and Station Etiquette.* Available at: https://www.electrifyamerica.com  
  *Context:* Outlines commercial rationale for enforcing idle fees ($0.40/min after 10-minute grace period) to deter overstaying and maintain station throughput.

### Foundational Game Theory Texts
- **[2] Shoham, Y., & Leyton-Brown, K.** (2008). *Multiagent Systems: Algorithmic, Game-Theoretic, and Logical Foundations.* Cambridge University Press.  
  - *Chapter 3:* Normal-Form Representation, Dominated Strategies, and Nash Equilibrium.
  - *Chapter 5:* Extensive-Form Games, Subgame Perfection, Information Sets, and Kuhn's Equivalence Theorem under Perfect Recall.
  - *Section 6.1:* Repeated Games, Folk Theorems, and Grim Trigger Strategies.
  - *Section 6.3:* Bayesian Games, Types, and Bayesian Nash Equilibrium.
  - *Section 7.7:* Evolutionary Game Theory, Replicator Dynamics, and Evolutionarily Stable Strategies (ESS).

### Empirical EV Datasets & Charging Models
- **[3] Lee, Z. J., Li, T., & Low, S. H.** (2019). ACN-Data: Analysis and Applications of an Open EV Charging Dataset. *Proceedings of the Tenth ACM International Conference on Future Energy Systems (e-Energy '19)*, 139–149. https://doi.org/10.1145/3307772.3328286  
  *Context:* Real-world traces of thousands of charging sessions at Caltech and JPL, demonstrating that vehicles remain plugged in long after battery saturation.

### Selected Research Paper for Project Review
- **[4] Wang, Y., Zhang, L., & Liu, X.** (2024). Evolutionary Game Analysis of Electric Vehicle Distribution Entities with Shared Charging Facilities. *Mathematics*, 12(21), 3413. https://doi.org/10.3390/math12213413  
  *Context:* Examines tripartite evolutionary dynamics between charging station operators, grid distribution utilities, and commercial EV fleets under carbon subsidies and dynamic tariff structures.
